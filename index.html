06 — Why BK Mentoring

“I'm looking for a mentor who will look closely at my ideas, my products and the way I work, and tell me plainly what to improve.”

Competitions and project teams have put me alongside many talented people. They have also helped me recognise the skills I want to strengthen: analysing problems more deeply and making better-informed decisions.

With more competitions and my own products ahead, this is the right moment to learn alongside an experienced mentor.
